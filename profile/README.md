## We turn uncertain software problems into working products your team can own.

German project leadership owns discovery, requirements, architecture, QA, and release decisions. Our Pakistani engineering team builds against the same brief and the same quality gates, from the first sprint through to handover — with daily overlap in your working hours.

The same model serves clients in Germany, the United States, and internationally.

> ### "One partner, full accountability, zero compromise."

<br>

## How we work

| Principle | What it means for you |
|:--|:--|
| **German-led delivery** | Clear ownership and senior engineering — never black-box outsourcing. You see the roadmap, not a status report. |
| **Vetted senior engineers only** | No juniors, no freelancers on your build. Every engineer is assigned deliberately. |
| **You own everything** | The code, the process, and the team. IP and licences assign to you on payment — zero grey areas. |
| **Written by default** | A German-led delivery lead, shared Slack or Teams, written weekly plans, and demos on a rhythm you choose. Critical decisions stay in writing. |

## Straight answers

<details>
<summary><b>Worried the quality won't hold up?</b></summary>

<br>

We only assign vetted senior engineers — no juniors, no freelancers. We delivered a full-stack MVP in six weeks for a Berlin startup. You see the roadmap, not a black box.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Think time zones will slow everything down?</b></summary>

<br>

We sync to your hours whether you're in Germany, the US, or elsewhere — full or partial overlap. Our Pakistan team works in lockstep with your operations, so no context gets lost.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Afraid you'll lose control of your product?</b></summary>

<br>

You always own the code, the process, and the team. Our BOT model lets you scale now and fully own later — with zero grey areas.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How does day-to-day communication work?</b></summary>

<br>

You get a German-led delivery lead, shared Slack or Microsoft Teams, written weekly plans, and demos on a rhythm you choose. Critical decisions stay in writing, so nothing gets lost between US, EU, and engineering hubs.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do pricing and contracts work?</b></summary>

<br>

We scope in phases with transparent estimates, typically quoted in EUR or USD depending on your entity and preference. You sign a clear SOW per phase — no open-ended "black box" retainers unless you explicitly want one.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can you sign an NDA, and who owns the IP?</b></summary>

<br>

Yes — mutual NDAs are standard before deep discovery. In our default model you own the IP and codebase; licences assign to you on payment per project terms. We align with how your legal team in the US or Germany needs it structured.

</details>
